---

## 1. The Consumer Zone (Public & Shopper)

These pages are designed for high conversion, buttery-smooth interactions, and clear financial tracking.

* **Landing Page:** A high-impact hero section using GSAP parallax to showcase the "Retail Nexus" ecosystem.
* **Global Marketplace:** The main feed where users can browse products from all vendors.
* **Comparison Engine Interface:** A specialized view where users can drop two or more products to compare quality scores and price history.
* **Product Detail Page (PDP):** Includes the "Save-to-Buy" (BNPL) toggle, product origin verification, and sustainability/resale options.
* **Consumer Dashboard:**
* **My Savings Progress:** A grid of active "Progress-to-Purchase" items with GSAP-animated bars.
* **Digital Wallet:** View balance from 95% refunds and loyalty points.
* **Order History:** Track live deliveries and "Pass-it-On" (Trade-in) options for old purchases.


* **Sustainability Hub:** A curated section for recycled or pre-owned verified goods.

---

## 2. The Vendor Zone (The Merchant Hub)

This is the "Operating System" for the shop owner. It switches between a high-utility POS and a data-rich business manager.

* **Pulse Dashboard:** An animated "Mission Control" showing real-time sales, low-stock alerts, and active BNPL customers.
* **Omni-Channel POS:**
* **Cashier View:** Optimized for tablet/touch. Works offline via SQLite.
* **Customer Lookup:** Quick-access loyalty card scanning and purchase history.


* **Inventory Manager:** * **Stock Tracking:** Add/Edit products and set "Min-Stock" alert thresholds.
* **Supplier Coordination:** Contact list and restock order generator.


* **Loyalty & Rewards Center:** Manage point systems and create "Secret Offers" for repeat customers.
* **Virtual Shop Customizer:** A simple CMS to change the shop's online appearance (banners, featured items).
* **Market Intelligence Page:** Competitive analysis showing how their prices stack up against the market median.

---

## 3. The Admin Zone (The Platform Owner)

Restricted to you as the platform owner to oversee the entire ecosystem.

* **Global Command Center:** Real-time map of all vendor locations and platform-wide revenue (GMV).
* **Vendor Onboarding & KYC:** A portal to approve or suspend vendors and review their business documents.
* **Financial Escrow Monitor:** Track all money currently sitting in "Save-to-Buy" progress jars and the 5% cancellation fees collected.
* **Dispute Resolution Center:** Manage refund requests or customer complaints regarding product quality.
* **System Health:** Monitoring the SQLite-to-Convex sync traffic and API usage.

---

## 4. Shared & Utility Pages

* **Auth Pages (Clerk):** Sign-in, Sign-up, and Role Selection (Merchant vs. Shopper).
* **User Profile:** Settings for notifications (Email/SMS), security, and contact info.
* **Help & Documentation:** * **SME Toolkit:** Simple guides for small kiosk owners on how to use the app.
* **Consumer FAQ:** Explaining the BNPL refund rules.


* **404 / Error Pages:** Animated with GSAP to keep the user experience delightful even when things go wrong.
